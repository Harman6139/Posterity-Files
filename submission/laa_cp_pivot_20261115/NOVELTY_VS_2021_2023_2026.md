The source result is one theorem in the ordinary CP_n cone for each fixed n>=5:
canonical initialization, every improving extreme direction, positive definite and
strictly entrywise positive nonmembers, explicit perturbation ball, all local
exits excluded, and a short explicit common separator. The independent geometric
component bounds cross-block movement along every bounded edge for any fixed
positive definite leading block. No blanket “first” claim is made.

The earlier project results used prescribed shadow rules, different starts, or
restricted column cones. They were inspected as background and are not invented
external publications or dependencies of the present self-contained proof.
The unrelated arithmetic-carrier and Lorentz results are excluded.
