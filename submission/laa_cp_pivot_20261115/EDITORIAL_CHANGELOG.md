## Preservation and imports

The original canonical n>=5 theorem remains Corollary 2.6. Appendix A preserves
the exact seed B, scale N=20000 n^3 E^2, normalization 4nN^2, perturbation radius
1/(1600 n^4 N^2), separator, norm/margin/pairing/distance reserves, canonical T_n
start, all-choice guarantee and fixed-order binary statement. Its scale is shown
to exceed the general confinement threshold; the original robustness arithmetic
is proved separately, rather than falsely identified with the new constants.

Imported results: certified-seed amplification with linear scale Gamma(m+1),
all starts in one fixed bounded region of the same face, robust common short
separator, stored extreme-direction diagonal-correction barrier, finite shear
types and rational denominators, connected bounded-degree graph with exact
degree-r intrinsic growth, separate full-lattice quasi-isometry, graphical seed
intervals, all-proper-principal CP on a ball, and branched D_r leading blocks.

Qualifications made explicit: m is an integer; stored directions are selected
improving extreme tangent rays; arbitrary real test coefficients are permitted
but the final candidate must be copositive; real positive-definite H suffices for
geometry, rational H for denominators and perfect H for full tangent rays.
Finite containing lists need not be realized types. Orthant shears are not
treated as graph automorphisms. Graph distances do not control paths outside
the face. Arbitrary algorithms, unrelated separators, nonadjacent moves,
non-descending steps and distant input-dependent starts are outside the model.

The graphical path condition is an even number of vertices (odd edge length).
The order-thirteen example uses the certificate JSON instance
delta=71/17220, a=73849/17220; the different valid prose instance was not silently
identified with it. Tree path indicators are a spanning family, not necessarily
the full minimal-vector list. The invalidated general triangular-book claim is
not used; only unaffected classical forest/small-block SPN facts are invoked.
