## The mathematical strengthening proved in this continuation

The preceding release gave a canonical arithmetic carrier theorem and an exponential unique SHADOW walk on interior MEMBERS. It did not prove a robust nonmember obstruction for EVERY descending extreme-ray local rule. The new proof supplies that stronger conclusion in every fixed dimension n >= 4, on one fixed full-dimensional pointed nonsimplicial rational column cone per dimension.

For d=n(n+1)/2 and any m>=1, set

    N=100(d-1)! [2(m+1)^2]^(d-1).

The constructed normalized rational target has O_n(log(m+1)) input bits. Every algorithm restricted to strict objective descent along extreme tangent rays from the specified fixed initial vertex must make at least m exact adjacent-vertex pivots. Every improving ray is unique. The target is positive definite and outside the constrained Gram cone. A fixed integral unimodular congruence makes all target entries strictly positive.

The conclusion persists on a Frobenius ball of radius 1/(30000 n^3 N^2). The same explicit rational separator works throughout that ball, with cone margin at least 3/(200 n^2 N^2), target pairing at most -1/(80 n N^2), and separation distance at least 1/(3600 n^2 N^2). Every ball target also strictly passes PSD and all pairwise facet-product tests. No scalar identity correction of the selected ray can supply a valid separator on either robust family, so a correct test restricted to such corrections is also forced to descend. The center has compact rational encoding; arbitrary rational perturbations in the ball need not have the same input bit length.

The separator has O_n(log N) bit height and can be constructed and verified in polynomial bit time from the family parameters. A matching single-exponential upper growth class for the fixed-anchor shadow rule follows from the existing sharp lattice estimate and an explicit anchor norm bound.

## Mechanism

A positive average of rank-one active normals exposes a tangent face. Primitive integral ray heights are bounded by cofactors. Away from that face, the average has a rational gap at least 1/10, and the N^2 part of the objective dominates every possible O(N) negative contribution. The entire surviving face is explicitly generated by two axial and 2(n-2) mixed rays. Exactly one has negative objective cost. Thus the proof covers all permitted local choices analytically, not by enumerating rules.
