\subsection{The natural facet-based relaxation also fails to detect the family}
The positivity assertion can be strengthened without changing the construction or local proof. Define the full facet map
\[
 Gx=(t+z_1,t-z_1,\ldots,t+z_r,t-z_r,y)\trans.
\]
Every matrix in $C(K)$ is PSD and has $GXG\trans$ entrywise nonnegative. These are stronger elementary tests than positivity of the matrix entries in one arbitrarily chosen coordinate system.

\begin{corollary}[Strict feasibility of the facet-product relaxation]\label{cor:facetrelaxation}
Every target in the ball \eqref{eq:allball} satisfies $A\succ0$ and has $GAG\trans$ strictly entrywise positive, but remains outside $C(K)$. Every target in \eqref{eq:allpositiveball} has the corresponding property for the facet map $GS^{-1}$ of $K'$. Thus the all-local lower bound survives positive-semidefinite preprocessing and all pairwise products of the defining facet inequalities.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
For $B_N$, same-coordinate facet products have values $(1+a)N^2$ or $(1-a)N^2$, different-coordinate products have value $N^2$, and products with $y$ have values $N(1\pm c_i)\ge N/10$, where $(c_1,c_2,c_3,\ldots)=(a,-a,0,\ldots)$. The last diagonal equals $h=29/10$. All entries of $GA_NG\trans$ are consequently at least $1/(20nN^2)$. Every row of $G$ has norm at most $\sqrt2$, so a perturbation in \eqref{eq:allball} changes an entry by at most $2\varepsilon_N<1/(20nN^2)$. Positive definiteness and nonmembership were already proved. For the transformed ball use
\[
 (GS^{-1})\widehat A(GS^{-1})\trans=\tfrac13G(A_N+\Delta)G\trans,
 \qquad\norm\Delta_F\le\varepsilon_N.
\]
This proves the stated robust strict positivity.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Scalar diagonal ray corrections cannot shortcut the prefix}
The local obstruction also survives the common option of testing a scalar identity correction of the selected ray, rather than requiring that ray itself to be copositive.
\begin{corollary}[No separating identity correction]\label{cor:raycorrection}
At every forced prefix vertex, no matrix of the form $F_k+\tau I$ is both $K$-copositive and negatively paired with a target in \eqref{eq:allball}. Likewise no $S^{-\mathsf T}F_kS^{-1}+\tau I$ is both $K'$-copositive and negatively paired with a target in \eqref{eq:allpositiveball}. This holds for every real $\tau$. Thus any correct descent-or-certificate test restricted to these scalar identity corrections must choose descent throughout the prefix.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Put $s=k+1$. The $(t,y)$ block of $F_k$ is $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&-1\\-1&2s\end{smallmatrix}\right)$. Its negative eigenvalue is $-\kappa$, where
\[
 \kappa=\sqrt{s^2+1}-s=\frac1{\sqrt{s^2+1}+s}\ge\frac1{3s}.
\]
A corresponding unit eigenvector has positive $t,y$ and $z=0$, hence lies in $K$. Therefore copositivity of $F_k+\tau I$ requires $\tau\ge\kappa$. For a perturbed target write $2nN^2A=\widetilde B$. The radius in \eqref{eq:allball} gives $\norm{\widetilde B-B_N}_F\le1/(1000n)$, whence
\[
 \tr\widetilde B\ge N^2/2,\qquad
 \ip{\widetilde B}{F_k}\ge-3N.
\]
Here $\norm{F_k}_F\le3s$ and $N\ge100s$. Consequently a copositive correction has pairing at least $-3N+N^2/(6s)>0$ before normalization.

For the transformed target put $6nN^2\widehat A=\widetilde C$ and $F'=S^{-\mathsf T}F_kS^{-1}$. The perturbation radius gives $\norm{\widetilde C-SB_NS\trans}_F\le1/(5000n^2)$. Since $\norm{F'}_F\le15ns$, and $\tr(SB_NS\trans)\ge(4.9r+2)N^2$, we have $\tr\widetilde C\ge nN^2$ and $\ip{\widetilde C}{F'}\ge-3N$. Evaluating $F'+\tau I$ at the image of the unit eigenvector above forces $\tau\ge\kappa/\norm S_2^2\ge1/(15ns)$. Its unnormalized target pairing is therefore at least
\[
 -3N+\frac{N^2}{15s}>0.
\]
This excludes all claimed separators. Rescaling the ray positively does not change the conclusion.
\end{proof}
