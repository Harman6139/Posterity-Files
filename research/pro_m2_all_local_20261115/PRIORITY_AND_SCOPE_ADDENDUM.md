# Priority and scope addendum — 15 November 2026

## Specific contribution isolated here

The previous carrier release proved a unique specified-shadow trajectory on rational interior members. This continuation proves a unique descending extreme ray of the entire feasible tangent cone at each of an exponentially long prefix of vertices. Consequently it covers every local rule confined to strict objective descent along extreme tangent rays. The inputs are robust positive-definite nonmembers of a fixed nonsimplicial constrained Gram cone, become strictly entrywise positive under a fixed integral congruence, and strictly pass every pairwise facet-product relaxation inequality. They have explicit strict global separators of logarithmic height. Scalar identity corrections of the selected rays cannot furnish a valid early separator anywhere in the robust prefix families.

The decisive mechanism is the interaction of a rank-one exposing average, the primitive integral cofactor bound on all extreme rays, and an exact finite generator description of the exposed tangent face. The abstract objective-dominance inequality itself is elementary and is not claimed as new general linear-programming theory. Nor are the Horn copositive form, its triangle-free graph proof, basic congruence transformations, or cofactor bounds claimed new.

## Primary antecedents

Dutour Sikirić–Schürmann–Vallentin, A simplex algorithm for rational cp-factorization, Mathematical Programming 187 (2021), 25–45, DOI 10.1007/s10107-020-01467-4: the simplex/perfect-form framework, rational cp certificates, and the suitable-pivot termination question. This paper does not formulate the present fixed-cone robust all-descending-rule lower bound.

Dannenberg–Schürmann, Perfect copositive matrices, Communications in Mathematics 31 (2023), 137–156, DOI 10.46298/cm.11141: structural shear and perfect-matrix geometry used by earlier project work. The new theorem does not claim first discovery of shear forms.

Oertel–Schürmann, Generalized Perfect Matrices, arXiv:2602.05841v2 (2026): generalized cones, perfect matrices, qualitative Interior Ryshkov theory, and rational certificate methods. Those qualitative generalizations are prior art, not the present contribution.

The inherited priority audit further credits Borwein–Wolkowicz, Safey El Din–Zhi, Hillar, Scheiderer, Laplagne, and the grid-approximation literature. Its discussion of the canonical arithmetic carrier is preserved separately. The new local theorem is independent of the correctness of reported 2026 irrational-boundary-factorization counterexamples.

## Interpretation of the general program

The canonical carrier controls admissible rational columns and exact completion. The new integral exposed-face argument shows why bounded arithmetic heights and short certificates do not make local walks short. It strengthens the local/global obstruction without asserting a false universal scalar condition-number equivalence or an impossibility theorem for arbitrary nonlocal algorithms.
