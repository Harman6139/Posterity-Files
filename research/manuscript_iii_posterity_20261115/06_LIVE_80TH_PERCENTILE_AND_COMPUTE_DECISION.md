# Follow-on benchmark: 80th-percentile feasibility and value of additional research threads
**Date:** 2026-11-15. This is an evidence update to [03_PUBLISHED_PAPER_BENCHMARK_2025.md](03_PUBLISHED_PAPER_BENCHMARK_2025.md) and [04_VENUE_DECISION_AND_NOVELTY.md](04_VENUE_DECISION_AND_NOVELTY.md).

## Verdict at the current proved theorem boundary

**No demonstrated upper-20% rank at JCA, SIOPT or MOR.** JCA has a credible fit, particularly for the complete PLQ spectral jump rigidity/nonrepresentability theorem and/or the exact-rational fixed-two-coordinate logarithmic minimax theorem. A careful focused manuscript could become *competitive with published JCA articles*, but top 20% requires proof-level independent mathematical reviews and convincing priority.

Original rational free-radius exact value-gradient dimension-uniform minimax objective gap: still only Omega(log(1/epsilon))<=N(epsilon)<=O(1/epsilon) at beta=1/2. Prior real-Haar theorem proves the sharper law only with REAL chart coefficients; positive error or excursion charging change the information model. No improvement here.

A research portfolio is not a single journal article; do not inflate quality by counting mutually incompatible theorem models as a solved "grand theorem." No acceptance probability is inferred.

## SIOPT 2025 comparisons with accessible author proof versions

- Klingler–Netzer, *Homotopy Methods for Convex Optimization*, published [DOI 10.1137/24M1693416](https://doi.org/10.1137/24M1693416), full 28-page author version https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.02095 inspected (principal ODE/path existence theorems and proof sections). The study derives a homotopy stationary ODE, existence/uniqueness of solution path under conditions, SDP/hyperbolic families, and numerical comparisons. Their mathematical and algorithmic reach is wider than the **current narrow conic exact-rational** result. If the *original* arithmetic minimax conjecture were fully resolved, relevance might improve dramatically.
- Liang–Huang–Xin–Yuan, *Understanding the Influence of Digraphs on Decentralized Optimization: Effective Metrics, Lower Bound, and Optimal Algorithm*, published [DOI 10.1137/24M1657341](https://doi.org/10.1137/24M1657341), 32-page author version https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.04928 inspected (the new equilibrium-skewness parameter, lower bound section and optimization method). This is a concrete benchmark for a complete original information-complexity theorem: **structural invariant + matching lower bound + algorithm + applications**. Our work matches this pattern only in restricted, mutually differentiated models, not the target rational all-pencil oracle.
- Ding–Wright, *On Squared-Variable Formulations*, published [DOI 10.1137/23M1608343](https://doi.org/10.1137/23M1608343). Publisher abstract confirms approximate second-order stationarity transfer under hypotheses and LP/QP/NMF experiments. A cautionary representation-vs-optimization comparator; not a direct priority conflict.
- Jeong–Sossa, *Commutation Principles for Nonsmooth Variational Problems on Euclidean Jordan Algebras*, published [DOI 10.1137/24M1646777](https://doi.org/10.1137/24M1646777). Broad Jordan-algebra nonsmooth commutation principle by publisher abstract. Our PLQ spectral result is more specialized; the published article's detailed proofs were not independently reviewed in this follow-on.

Other eight SIOPT predeclared sample articles are still in the earlier 32-paper benchmark, mainly with verified abstract/TOC rather than full-proof access. **No claim to exceed 10/12 papers at an 80th-percentile bar**.

## MOR 2025 comparisons with accessible author proof versions

- Grand-Clément–Petrik, *On the Convex Formulations of Robust Markov Decision Processes*, published in MOR 50(3), [DOI 10.1287/moor.2022.0284](https://doi.org/10.1287/moor.2022.0284), author version https://arxiv.org/pdf/2209.10187 inspected (Bellman and convex reformulation arguments, decision applications). Proves convex formulations of robust MDPs under classic rectangularity assumptions, with concrete conic tractability reductions. It has stronger mainstream OR relevance than a pure hidden-coefficient SDP oracle theorem.
- Royset–Chen–Eckstrand, *Rockafellian Relaxation and Stochastic Optimization Under Perturbations*, MOR 50(3), [DOI 10.1287/moor.2022.0122](https://doi.org/10.1287/moor.2022.0122), author version https://arxiv.org/pdf/2204.04762 inspected (exact/limit-exact relaxations, perturbation convergence and proof sections). Broad nonconvex nonsmooth model plus stochastic and real application demonstrations. Our measurement error thresholds are a much narrower oracle problem.
- Remaining eight MOR published article comparisons and DOIs are in the original 32-paper sample; most were assessed only to abstract level. The 2026 [MOR editorial statement](https://pubsonline.informs.org/page/moor/editorial-statement) explicitly requires mathematical originality and relevance to mainstream OR/decision-making. An exact value/gradient information theorem needs clear operational significance to improve fit.

## 2026 nearest topical control (NOT a published-in-selected-issues observation)

Dadush–Végh–Zambelli, *On finding exact solutions of linear programs in the oracle model*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11820 (June 2026 preprint), gives polynomial-in-(dimension, log condition) separation-oracle exact LP recovery. The conic model here is different, but this illustrates how a major exact-arithmetic oracle result makes model, condition and bit complexity operational. It is NOT direct evidence of overlap with our PSD theorems.

## What would move the current work into a defensible top-quintile contest?

1. A self-contained proof verified by a skeptical independent mathematical referee **and** a documented priority check against spectral calculus, weakly infeasible SDP literature, matrix-sketching and PLQ representation classification. No proven theorem currently overrides this proof/priority gate.
2. A single coherent significant theorem with sharp examples and the matching algorithm under EXACTLY the same oracle interface. The existing **two-coordinate logarithmic** class achieves this, but its generality and practical relevance need stronger framing. Alternatively the PLQ jump propagation result has broad geometry but needs proof-independent validity and originality review.
3. To compete near top of SIOPT/MOR: solve the original all-dimensional rational exact-oracle minimax gap OR prove a comparably powerful universal correction, with consequences for recognizable SDP/OR algorithms beyond an idealized hidden-matrix instrument. Add finite-precision/height implications in a correctly stated model. Avoid claiming this is necessary for JCA ordinary acceptance.

A **top-quintile estimate cannot be justified by dividing the counts of comparison papers that look shorter, different or more specialized**; cross-subfield theorem-depth ranking requires expert referees. No acceptance percentage is inferred.

## Should more compute/model threads be used?

**Conditional YES, bounded and hypothesis-diverse, not a large parallel brute-force campaign.** The frontier is conceptual: free exact algebraic queries defeat rational quadratic hard instances; scalar positive moment masks dissipate energy exponentially; noncommuting PSD branches still lack a proven polynomial-energy sequential-hiding mechanism. Running dozens of model instances through the SAME quadratic/hiding prompt is likely low marginal value.

Recommended initial portfolio of at most four independent rigorous research roles:
- **Positive theorem architect (1):** construct high-dimensional rational PSD spectral branch masking with exact arbitrary-query locality and a *polynomial* optimization barrier. Must prove common all-scale escape, isometry, fixed prior and arbitrary-output quantifiers; failure of any is failure.
- **Negative theorem architect (1):** prove a dimension-uniform strong exact-oracle recovery/optimization algorithm across all rational PSD spectral strata, allowing rank-changing branches and unrestricted q. A fixed-dimension analytic-identity trick is insufficient.
- **Novelty + adversarial mathematical referee (1):** independently check our entire strongest PLQ proof and exact rational binomial proof; search 2024–2026 priority; produce explicit counterexamples to every overstrong lemma. This has high direct value even with no new frontier proof.
- **Conic applications/manuscript writer (1):** extract a JCA-scope finished paper, including full proofs, relationship to known exact-relaxation/PLQ work, examples, and meaningful algorithmic meaning. Additional weaker models/short computations are used only after a concrete analytic lemma needs falsification.

Use best available High/Extra-High or similarly deep reasoning efforts only when plan access permits. **Decision gate:** continue spending heavily on the flagship track only if one independent architect delivers an actual proved, nontrivial universal-direction lemma or a counterexample that changes the landscape. If not, stop duplicating attempts and put effort into referee audit plus publication of completed limited-scope results.

### Bottom line

There is a reasonable research case for **a small, independently assigned stronger-model portfolio** because a genuine theorem would change the venue ambition. There is **not** evidence to fund arbitrary parallel compute on the premise that the whole frontier is already almost solved. For the nearer JCA opportunity, proof auditing and paper assembly probably have better marginal value than more brute-force proof-route searches. **Current top-20% venue standing is not certified at any of the sampled venues.**
