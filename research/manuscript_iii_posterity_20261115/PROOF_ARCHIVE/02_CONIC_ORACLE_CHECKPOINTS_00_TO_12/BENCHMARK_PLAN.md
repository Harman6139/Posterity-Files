# Predeclared published-paper benchmark

Date: 2026-11-14. This plan is recorded before opening the sampled issue tables of contents.

Primary sample: the first five research articles, in the publisher's table-of-contents order, in each of SIAM Journal on Optimization 35(3) and 35(4) (2025), and Mathematics of Operations Research 50(3) and 50(4) (2025). Exclude front matter, corrections and editorials; record exclusions. This produces ten articles per journal from two complete issues of the most recently completed calendar year. Add nearest-neighbor topical articles separately; do not replace inconvenient sample articles.

Record title, DOI, theorem scope, available full-text/proof access, and relevance. Compare correctness, novelty, breadth, proof depth, applications and exposition. Publisher-blocked articles must be marked as such; abstract-only review is not full-proof review. A provisional upper-quintile assertion requires at least eight defensible favorable comparisons among ten AND all essential proof and priority gates. No editorial acceptance probability will be inferred.

Essential target gate: the original handoff requires rationally described weakly infeasible SDP instances, a fixed distribution before the algorithm's seed, unrestricted exact value/gradient queries and output, dimension-uniform escape geometry, and matching information bounds. A real-coefficient theorem or a bounded-query/noisy rational theorem must be labeled separately and cannot by itself pass this original gate.
