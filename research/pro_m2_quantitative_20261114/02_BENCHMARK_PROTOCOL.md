This protocol is fixed before comparative scores or pass/fail labels are assigned. It is a research-quality audit, not an acceptance-probability model.

## Evidence and dimensions

For each scored paper record DOI, publication metadata, full-text access, exact main result, hypotheses, proof mechanism, quantitative strength, limitations, and the precise overlap with this manuscript. Assess correctness (internal audit, not claimed independent peer review), novelty relative to closest antecedents, theorem depth, breadth, constructive usefulness, fit, and exposition. Abstract-only metadata is insufficient for a substantive score.

A statement that our work passes against 8 of 10 papers requires 10 complete evidence records and a documented comparison rather than invented numerical precision. Even 8 of 10 in this small sample is a sample comparison, not statistical certification of a population percentile. A venue recommendation and any handling-time data must be separately justified. Missing evidence yields NOT CERTIFIED, never an inferred pass.
