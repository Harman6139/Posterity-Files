## Primary question and current standing

Can every rational trace-controlled real SDP be represented by TWO orthonormal-basis PSD diagonals with ambient N=O(m²+p), exact or polynomially condition-controlled approximate recovery, and positive efficient decoding?

**Unresolved in full.** The strong exact full-fibre positive-linear retract model is generically impossible at quadratic size: a proved lower bound is N=Omega(m^(8/3)). The unrestricted many-to-one quotient model is not ruled out. Special-class quadratic exact lifts and the trace-only genuine two-basis quotient have been proved.

The latest complementary advances are:
- Milestone 15: a quadratic symmetric-square PSD marginal extension with no positive linear section;
- Milestone 16: why that extension cannot carry a fixed-basis diagonal over every source pure state;
- Milestone 17 and corrected Milestone 20: exact LINEAR fixed-first-basis two-MASA compatibility test, and dimension-independent no-go for any maximally entangled first basis with two noncommuting source observables;
- Milestone 19: a genuine order-2m² two-basis shadow of the full trace-one state cone, with mixed pure-state lifts and Theta(m^4) decoder-invisible slack.
