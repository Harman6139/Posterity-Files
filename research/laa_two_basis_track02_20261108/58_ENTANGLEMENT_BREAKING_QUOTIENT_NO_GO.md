# Milestone 58 — an explicit bounded trace-controlled SDP that NO finite entanglement-breaking decoder can represent

Research Track 02, 2026-11-09. This is a rigorous obstruction to a broad surviving many-to-one scheme: using an entanglement-breaking / measure-and-prepare positive decoder to turn quantum coherence in an arbitrary large two-basis PSD fibre into source matrices. It assumes NO source encoder, no bijective exact fibres, no restriction on the lifted basis design or size, and no trace-preservation of the decoder. The obstruction is output CONE GEOMETRY, not operator-system commutator algebra. It does NOT apply to general CP decoders with coherent Kraus contributions, to positive non-CP decoders, or to nonlinear decoding.

## Theorem A — open families of singular supports exclude finite-output EB maps

Let F be a subset of S^m_+ containing a family of rank-(m−1) matrices X_u for every projective kernel direction [u] in a nonempty open subset U⊂RP^(m−1), with
  ker X_u=span(u).
Let
  D:S^N→S^m
be any real entanglement-breaking CP map with a FINITE measure-and-prepare representation
  D(Y)=sum_(j=1)^T <M_j,Y> E_j,
  M_j>=0 in S^N, E_j>=0 in S^m.
Then F is NOT a subset of D(S^N_+), for ANY finite N or T. In particular F cannot be the decoder image of any exact two-orthonormal-basis PSD feasible fibre.

**Proof.** For every Y>=0, the coefficients <M_j,Y> are nonnegative, so D(Y) belongs to the finitely generated PSD cone C=cone(E_1,...,E_T).

If a rank-(m−1) PSD matrix X_u with kernel span(u) belongs to C, write X_u=sum_j lambda_j E_j with lambda_j>=0. Taking the quadratic form on u gives
  0=u^T X_u u=sum_j lambda_j u^T E_j u.
All summands are nonnegative. Therefore at least one NONZERO E_j with lambda_j>0 must satisfy u^T E_j u=0. For PSD E_j this is equivalent to E_j u=0, i.e. u belongs to the fixed proper linear subspace ker E_j of R^m.

Thus the projective kernel directions of rank-(m−1) matrices in C lie in
  union_(j:E_j!=0) P(ker E_j),
a FINITE union of proper projective linear subspaces. Such a finite union has empty interior in RP^(m−1). It cannot contain U. Contradiction. QED.

This uses only FINITENESS of the output PSD preparation list, not the size of the input or the number of diagonal observations.

## Theorem B — explicit rational trace-controlled source problem with an open kernel-normal band

Fix any integer m>=3. Put a=1+t, b=1−t/(m−1), and define the affine m-by-m symmetric matrix pencil
  X(t,v) = [[1+t, v^T],
            [v, (1−t/(m−1)) I_(m−1)]],
where t∈R and v∈R^(m−1). Every X(t,v) has trace m.

Let A:S^m→R^(D_m−m) be any rational linear operator whose kernel is the m-dimensional trace-zero 'arrow' space
  V={ [[t, v^T],
         [v, −t/(m−1) I_(m−1)]] : t∈R, v∈R^(m−1) },
and set b0=A(I_m). Then the nonempty compact bounded trace-controlled SDP fibre
  F_b0={X>=0:A(X)=b0}
is exactly {X(t,v)>=0}.

For all sufficiently small real |t|, both a,b are positive. Choose
  v=sqrt(a b) w,  w in S^(m−2).
The Schur complement of the lower-right positive block bI gives
  a−||v||²/b=0,
so X(t,v) has rank m−1 and kernel generated by
  u(t,w)=(1, −sqrt(a/b) w).
The map t -> sqrt[(1+t)/(1−t/(m−1))] has strictly positive derivative at t=0. Therefore the projective kernel directions [u(t,w)] as t varies near zero and w varies on S^(m−2) contain a nonempty open annular band in RP^(m−1).

Theorem A now proves that NO finite entanglement-breaking CP decoder, regardless of its input PSD order or its two-basis measurements, can project an exact lifted PSD feasible fibre onto F_b0. This example is entirely rational at the level of the input measurement operator and source data; the individual boundary witnesses may have algebraic coefficients.

A generic common rational orthogonal/congruence change of source coordinates can make the measurement matrices dense/noncommuting while preserving the open-kernel-boundary obstruction (orthogonal changes preserve ranks and kernel openness). The explicitly displayed arrow pencil already suffices.

## Theorem C — a STRICT, architecture-wide weak-recovery error floor at fixed number of prepared states

Fix m>=3, choose a compact subfamily K of the rank-(m−1) boundary band in Theorem B whose projective kernel directions contain a nonempty open subset U_0 with compact closure inside the band, and fix a positive integer T.

There is a number eps_(m,T,K)>0 such that for EVERY choice of T normalized PSD output preparation matrices E_j>=0 with tr E_j=1, one has
  max_(X∈K) dist_*(X,cone(E_1,...,E_T)) >=eps_(m,T,K).   (1)
Consequently any entanglement-breaking decoder with at most T nonzero prepared PSD effects fails to approximate ALL source matrices X∈K in nuclear norm to an error below eps_(m,T,K), even with arbitrary lift selection and arbitrarily large input PSD dimension.

**Proof by compactness.** The set of T-tuples of normalized density matrices E_j>=0,trE_j=1 is compact. Suppose no positive eps existed. There would be tuples E^(n) with
  sup_(X∈K) dist_*(X,C_n) ->0,
C_n=cone(E_1^(n),...,E_T^(n)).
Extract a convergent subsequence E_j^(n)->E_j. For each fixed X∈K choose an approximant Z_n=sum_j lambda_(n,j) E_j^(n) with ||X−Z_n||_*->0. Since all E_j^(n) have trace 1 and X has fixed trace m, the nonnegative coefficients satisfy
  sum_j lambda_(n,j)=tr Z_n<=m+o(1).
They are bounded, so after a further subsequence depending on X converge to lambda_j>=0. Passing to the limit gives X=sum_j lambda_j E_j. Thus the single LIMIT cone C=cone(E_1,...,E_T) contains EVERY X∈K. This contradicts Theorem A because K has an open set of rank-(m−1) kernel directions. Therefore eps>0 exists. QED.

The constant is compactness-derived and may be tiny; no polynomial-in-m,T rate is claimed. It nevertheless shows that a fixed finite-outcome measure-and-prepare decoder cannot achieve arbitrarily high uniform approximate recovery accuracy on this explicit SDP, even if its two bases or input PSD dimension vary with tolerance. If the number T of prepared output effects grows with requested accuracy, the theorem does NOT provide a uniform error floor.

## Interpretation and audit

- 'Entanglement breaking' here explicitly means a finite measure-and-prepare decomposition with PSD input effects M_j and PSD output states E_j. This is the standard finite-dimensional separable-Choi subclass of real CP maps. We make no claim that every CP map has such a form.
- The input SDP is trace controlled because the arrow tangent space has trace zero, hence I_m belongs to range(A*). It is bounded because all feasible X have fixed trace m.
- The boundary kernel directions form a genuinely OPEN set, not just infinitely many points; the latter alone would not exclude a finite union of projective hyperplanes.
- The image cone bound is independent of whether lifted PSD competitors have rank one or high rank, whether the two measured ONBs differ, or whether the lifted feasible fibre is singular.
- The exact impossibility is for one EXPLICIT family of source SDPs. This is enough to exclude the measure-and-prepare decoder architecture as a UNIVERSAL normal form; it is not an impossibility for all bounded SDPs or all CP decoders.
- The quantitative fixed-T statement is an ERROR FLOOR for output approximation in nuclear norm, not a claim about residual-based SDP feasibility or complexity classes.
- The strongest remaining candidate for an unrestricted O(m²+p) two-basis representation must keep genuinely quantum/coherent Kraus terms, allow non-CP positive maps, or use a nonlinear decoder. The full frontier remains open.
