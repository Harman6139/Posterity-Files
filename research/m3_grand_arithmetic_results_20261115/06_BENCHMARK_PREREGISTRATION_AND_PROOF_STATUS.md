## What is actually proved in this branch

01: n-query exact function identification versus zero-query near-feasibility in a rational isometric weakly infeasible family with the same uniform escape profile.
02: a height-independent algebraic germ fingerprint and one-query objective recovery for rational globally constant-rank pencils; a finite-family value/first-order query equivalence.
03: rational everywhere-nonsingular weak-infeasibility examples with beta=(2^n-1)/2, isometric charts, explicit all-perturbation distance bounds, and one-query recovery despite slow prescribed projection dynamics.
04: explicit one-query height/precision upper and lower bounds for integer/rational matrix identification, including uniformly well-conditioned symmetric positive definite matrices. This is identification, not linear-system or conic optimization complexity.
05: spectral-chamber continuation, one-query recovery in the absence of codimension-one rank loss, a non-C2 weakly infeasible example in that class, and a worst-case sharp chamber-count law for GLOBAL IDENTIFICATION with a known chamber envelope.

These proofs use established quantifier elimination, analytic continuation, positional coding, and elementary counting. The new combinations require a serious priority audit. They have not received independent external mathematical review.

## What remains unresolved

A positive solution must use separating rank-changing branches to conceal the LOCATION of near-feasible points for T arbitrary finite exact queries, not merely conceal irrelevant coefficients. A negative solution must give a universally budgeted optimizer for those rank-changing cases. Neither is supplied by 01--05.

No quantitative bit-height phase diagram for the entire conic optimization class has been proved. The old positive-error conic theorem allows the prior and coefficient height to change as the positive error shrinks. The old real-coefficient theorem remains separate. No statement about a particular floating-point eigensolver follows from an adversarial measurement model.

The intended main.tex will be labeled as a research manuscript of PARTIAL STRUCTURAL RESULTS, not as a submission-ready resolution of the grand theorem, unless that missing theorem is actually proved later.
