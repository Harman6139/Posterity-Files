# Milestone 29 — multiaction obstruction at (nullity,depth)=(4,4)

**2026-11-08; proved for the edge-incidence class defined here, NOT for arbitrary dense kernels.**

## Setting

Use vertices 0,1,2,3,4. Source 0 is the rank-one target E00, and 4 is terminal. For each internal i=1,2,3 let E_i be a finite nonempty collection of unordered edges (a,b) with a,b distinct from i. Require all these edges, across all i, to be distinct and different from the optional leading edge (0,t). Let K be spanned by H_(i,ab)=Eii-E00+lambda Fab, for every action (a,b) in E_i, and G=lambda F_(0,t) when present; L projects onto K-perp. All generators have trace zero and terminal diagonal zero.

**Exact exposing closure for safe actions.** Let w0=0<w1<w2<w3<w4=1 satisfy 2w_i<=w_a+w_b for every action at i. At any remaining coordinate face W containing the source, a subset C of non-source remaining vertices can be removed by a PSD exposing stress if and only if, for every i in C and every parent edge (a,b) in E_i whose two endpoints remain, one has a,b in C. Necessity: the stress equations Zii+2lambda Zab=0 and PSD imply nonzero Zab forces both diagonal endpoints strictly positive. Sufficiency: put Zii=eta^(-w_i²) on C and zero on other remaining coordinates; choose Zab=-Zii/(2lambda) for within-face edges at i in C, and zero for remaining inactive edges; use unrestricted off-face entries to fulfill other equations. Because 2w_i<=w_a+w_b and w_a!=w_b, after diagonal normalization the within-face offdiagonal entries have magnitude (2lambda)^(-1) eta^((w_a²+w_b²)/2-w_i²) ->0. Thus the compressed stress is PSD, strictly positive on C. The leading edge (0,t) adds no constraint since PSD stresses have zero source row.

**Replacement-policy PSD arc.** Select one action per internal node, and solve 2u_i=u_a+u_b, u0=0,u4=1. If all u_i>0, set selected action coefficients x_i=s^(u_i), terminal diagonal s, source 1, optional leading coefficient z=s^(u_t/2), all other actions zero. Diagonal congruence normalizes its selected offdiagonal entries to lambda, so for lambda<1/10 it is PSD. Trace normalization gives residual Theta(s) and displacement at least Omega(s^(u_t/2)), thus any containing kernel has exponent at most u_t/2. If a selected policy has no edge reaching terminal, choose all internal diagonals equal to a small fixed c>0, terminal zero, and source 1-3c; normalized offdiag entries are small, giving a distinct exactly feasible PSD competitor and destroying uniqueness.

## Three sharp base networks

A: alpha=3/16, weights (3/8,1/2,3/4), actions E1=(0,3), E2=(0,4), E3=(2,4), and t=1.

B: alpha=3/16, weights (1/4,3/8,1/2), actions E1=(0,3), E2=(1,3), E3=(0,4), and t=2.

C: alpha=3/14, weights (3/7,5/7,6/7), actions E1=(0,3), E2=(1,4), E3=(2,4), and t=1.

These are the three exact-rational sparse sharp certificates listed in checkpoint 27.

## Theorem: arbitrary collision-free extra edge actions do not yield depth four at the original exponent

Call an additional edge at i **safe** if w_a+w_b>=2w_i and **unsafe** otherwise.

Even when all safe extra edges are included at once, the closure criterion produces just three exposing layers:

- A: {4},{3},{1,2}.
- B: {4},{3},{1,2}.
- C: {4},{2,3},{1}.

Thus every subset of safe edges has singularity degree <=3. In particular, a depth-four extension needs an unsafe edge. Exhaustive rational enumeration of unsafe edges not already present and distinct from (0,t) gives these exact forced arcs:

A, alpha=3/16:
 (i,edge)=(1,(0,2)): u_t/2=1/8;
 (3,(0,2)): 1/16;
 (3,(1,2)): 1/12;
 (3,(1,4)): 1/6.

B, alpha=3/16:
 (2,(0,1)): 1/16;
 (3,(0,1)) and (3,(1,2)): a policy never using terminal, giving an exactly feasible PSD matrix distinct from E00.

C, alpha=3/14:
 (1,(0,2)): 1/6;
 (2,(0,4)): 3/16;
 (2,(1,3)): 1/5;
 (3,(0,2)): 1/14;
 (3,(0,4)): 1/8;
 (3,(1,2)): 1/10.

Every displayed proper exponent is strictly smaller than its base target. A problem containing an unsafe edge therefore either loses uniqueness or acquires a feasible PSD perturbation arc violating the proposed exponent. Combining with the safe case proves the theorem.

**Audit:** A self-contained Fraction-arithmetic enumerator is supplied separately. The proof uses neither random numerical optimization nor any asserted dense-to-sparse correspondence.

**Limitation:** Arbitrary dense PSD kernels need not be generated by such independent edge actions. They may mix diagonal variables, use off-support matrix blocks, or have rotated facial flags. This theorem excludes only the full natural edge-extension class.
