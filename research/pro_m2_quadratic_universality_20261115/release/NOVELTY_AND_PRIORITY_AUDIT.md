# Priority and scope audit — 15 November 2026

This is a bounded primary-source comparison, not a certified historical first.
The earlier fixed LAA cohort remains unscored. The present research does not
replace inaccessible cohort papers with convenient examples or assert a
journal-wide percentile.

## Closest antecedents and exact distinctions

### Sturm–Zhang (2003): real rank-one generation

J. F. Sturm and S. Zhang, *On cones of nonnegative quadratic functions*,
Mathematics of Operations Research 28, 246–267.
DOI: 10.1287/moor.28.2.246.14485.
Primary manuscript: https://optimization-online.org/2001/05/324/ .

The real PSD/trace description for one quadratic constraint and related
rank-one decompositions are antecedents. The present paper includes an
independent elementary balancing proof and makes no first-real-cone claim.
Its central additional question is rational permitted factors, including
anisotropic trace-zero faces and exact affine rational completion.

### Nishijima–Lourenço (2026): second-order-cone real faces

M. Nishijima and B. F. Lourenço, *Facial structure of copositive and completely
positive cones over a second-order cone*, LAA 743, 211–249.
DOI: 10.1016/j.laa.2026.04.019.
Full primary version: https://arxiv.org/html/2502.04006v2 .
The v2 is dated 16 April 2026; current journal metadata was checked.

This paper is a substantial real facial-geometry comparator. We do not
claim to subsume its face classification, exposedness results or face-chain
calculations. Our restricted quadratic-form arithmetic identifies when
rational matrices on these real faces admit rational columns. The rank-five
consequence uses the classical Meyer theorem, not a newly proved arithmetic
local–global principle.

### Safey El Din–Zhi (2010): rational convex points

M. Safey El Din and L. Zhi, *Computing rational points in convex semi-algebraic
sets and sum of squares decompositions*, SIAM Journal on Optimization 20,
2876–2889. DOI: 10.1137/090772459.
Primary full text: https://perso.lip6.fr/Mohab.Safey/Articles/convex_sas.pdf .
Theorem 1.1 and its input model and bounds were inspected.

Rational convex-point decision is a charged existing primitive. It does not
by itself produce rational cone-constrained Gram columns. The maximal
rational support construction and the subsequent single-isotropy test
provide the additional factorization criterion. No new unrestricted
rational-SOS decision algorithm is claimed.

### Classical rational quadratic forms and explicit heights

J. W. S. Cassels, *Rational Quadratic Forms* (1978); and *Bounds for the least
solutions of homogeneous quadratic equations*, Proc. Cambridge Philos. Soc.
51 (1955), 262–264.
L. Fukshansky, *Heights and quadratic forms: Cassels’ theorem and its
generalizations*, Contemporary Mathematics 587 (2013), 77–94.
DOI: 10.1090/conm/587/11673.
Primary full text: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1204.1938 .
Theorem 1.1, including the explicit constant, was read and visually checked.
R. Dietmann, Canadian Mathematical Bulletin 52 (2009), 63–65,
DOI: 10.4153/CMB-2009-007-7, was checked as an additional height antecedent.

Hasse–Minkowski, Meyer, hyperbolic parametrization, and the Cassels small-zero
bound are classical. The unrestricted rank+3 rational Gram-width estimate
is a direct classical consequence, not a novelty claim. The paper connects
these facts to exact constrained factors and arbitrary affine carriers.

### Rational orthogonal approximation

E. Schmutz, *Rational points on the unit sphere*, Central European Journal
of Mathematics 6 (2008), 482–487, DOI: 10.2478/s11533-008-0038-4.
H. Liebeck and A. Osborn, *The generation of all rational orthogonal
matrices*, American Mathematical Monthly 98 (1991), 131–133.
Q. Tang, W. Wang and H. Zhang, *Generating all regular rational orthogonal
matrices*, LAA (2025), DOI: 10.1016/j.laa.2025.06.008.
Primary accessible text: https://arxiv.org/html/2410.11246v1 .
Theorem 3 there explicitly recalls Liebeck–Osborn's signed Cayley result.

These are antecedents for density and rational parametrizations. Our
open-domain width equality is proved from orbit transitivity, density and
positive rescaling, with an explicit height/clearance estimate. We do not
claim to have discovered a new rational parametrization of the orthogonal
group. The full 1991 and 2008 source proofs were not independently audited
in this continuation; the signed identity used here has a complete proof
in the manuscript.

### Burer–Dong (2012): conic representations of nonconvex QCQPs

S. Burer and H. Dong, *Representing quadratically constrained quadratic
programs as generalized copositive programs*, Operations Research Letters
40, 203–206. DOI: 10.1016/j.orl.2012.02.001.
Primary author PDF: https://sburer.github.io/papers/036-coposqcqp.pdf .
The full four-page theorem/proof text was inspected, including the product
second-order and PSD constructions and boundedness hypotheses.

Generalized CP representations of polynomial/quadratic optimization are
not new. Our two-observation circuit construction isolates exact rational
feasibility, needs no boundedness promise, keeps every small cone a PSD2
block, and shows that unrestricted factor width cannot escape the encoded
condition. The variable-dimensional exponential coordinate-height example
is a further explicit consequence. Historical priority of this precise
formulation is not certified.

### Earlier project and 2026 perfect matrices

A. Oertel and A. Schürmann, *Generalized Perfect Matrices*,
https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.05841v2 .
Current metadata and the qualitative rational-cone results were checked.
The earlier project already proves polyhedral arithmetic carriers and
robust exponential local adjacency paths. Those are inherited, not new
claims of this paper. The present domain is curved and may be nonconvex;
the exact isotropy condition replaces linear coefficient-kernel reduction.
No existing local-pivot lower bound is silently transferred to Lorentz
cones or to arbitrary algorithms.

## Claims whose proof is internal but first priority is not certified

The full single-quadratic rational factor criterion; its one-isotropy affine
carrier; the exact rational moment criterion and rank-four obstruction;
the complete open-domain width law with stated resources; and the precise
two-observation rational circuit reduction are the claims isolated for
further independent comparison. Search nonappearance is not proof of
novelty, and no numerical novelty or journal score is assigned.

## Deliberate exclusions

No universal rational boundary factorization of orthant CP matrices is
asserted. No dependence on unverified 2026 orthant counterexample data is
needed. No present decidability or undecidability claim is made about
arbitrary rational polynomial feasibility. No external human or formal
verification is claimed. The example programs do not implement the
abstract rational-point or isotropy algorithms.
