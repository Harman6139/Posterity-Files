# Rank universality and field-dependent tangency of symmetric cones

This is one consolidated research manuscript with three headline results: rank-universal central/coarse spectra, complete complex/quaternionic relative-flag spectra, and the infinite field/source separation after central orders are erased.

## Read and build

- `manuscript.pdf`: compiled 22-page pre-submission manuscript in the downloadable package; generated by `build.py` from the GitHub sources. The binary PDF is not tracked in the research branch.
- `manuscript.tex`, `sections/*.tex`, `references.bib`: complete corresponding source.
- `AUDIT_AND_PUBLICATION_MEMO.md`: adversarial dependency audit, actual correction, primary-literature comparison, and publication recommendation.
- `supplement/`: reproducibility programs and exact results.

Run `python3 build.py` with a TeX distribution containing pdflatex and BibTeX. This chooses `bibtex.original` or `bibtex8` if the normal executable is unavailable. Run `python3 supplement/independent_audit.py --output supplement/independent_audit.json` for the new independent finite audit. Other exact commands are in `supplement/README.md`.

## Correction and scope

The weighted cut-rank jump requires an internal grade `0 < tau < 1`. The unrestricted terminal version in the original Milestone 139 note is false; the counterexample and repair are explicitly recorded in the paper and audit. The unweighted cut lemma and the 5/27 field-separation theorem are unaffected. The original Milestone 139 remains in the historical project packet; its correction is recorded here rather than silently changing the archived statement. The four optional minimum-nullity witnesses are extracted separately in `supplement/finite_minimality_witnesses.md`.

All principal proofs are written out; no unpublished milestone is used as a black-box proof. The complete unrestricted real-profile and arbitrary-product classifications are not claimed and are not dependencies of the three principal theorems. Nullity-six minimality is separately computer-assisted and optional.
