# Research update to PL

## New theorems with complete proofs

**Reusable amplification.** A rational positive definite perfect leading block H, a positive rank-one normal reserve for the seed's top block, and an exact copositive separator suffice to generate robust exponential improving adjacent paths for ordinary CP nonmembers. This covers every improving rule and any fixed bounded portfolio of starts in the same face. Finite shear types improve the scale to N=Gamma(m+1), with all constants explicit, versus the previous higher-power sufficient scale. The original canonical theorem is retained unchanged.

**Broader correction barrier.** Arbitrary diagonal corrections of any prefix ray or any real combination of stored rays cannot separate, because every copositive arrow candidate is SPN. Fixed SPN-support candidate searches also fail. The SPN theorem is classical; confinement of every local direction makes it apply simultaneously here. For the collective families, every proper principal submatrix is CP on a certified ball, so even exact tests on all proper blocks and conic sums of such certificates fail.

**Qualifying classes.** An exact weighted path-forest-plus-hub construction provides long odd cycles, multiple overlapping attachments, bouquets, and arbitrarily large principal-minimal nonmembers, not padded five-cycle examples. Two seven-cycles sharing a hub yield order 13 with no smaller principal nonmember. Tree-path normal bases also give branched D_r perfect blocks; a D_7/order-eight seed was independently checked. Classical Schur/comparison criteria and bordered-diagonal antecedents are explicitly credited.

**Independent geometry.** For every SPD H of order r, the fixed-block lattice epigraph has finite shear-normal types, uniform active denominators, bounded degree, and intrinsic graph balls of exact growth Theta(R^r). The full-lattice graph is quasi-isometric to Z^r. Rational H gives one common denominator for all vertices, while intrinsic distances can be linear in M at logarithmic encoding height. These results require no hard nonmember objective.

## Objectives and restrictions

Objective 1 succeeded as a sufficient-conditions theory with several genuinely different qualifying classes. It is not a classification of every hard objective. Objective 2 succeeded for arbitrary diagonal corrections, stored-ray combinations, proper-principal tests, and same-face bounded portfolios. Arbitrary different-leading-block starts and nonlocal mechanisms remain outside the theorem. Objective 3 succeeded with the graph-growth/finite-type theorem. Singular-H, triangle, same-color, and missing-normal-reserve counterexamples delimit the statements.

## Verification and editorial significance

Fresh checks: eight seed families, 65 proper-principal certificates, 18 scaling cases, 20 geometric vertices, 142 active identities, 128 face rays, seven full-lattice templates, three arrow decompositions, and nine corruptions rejected. The original suite was rerun without source changes. The universal statements are analytical, not inferred from these finite tests.

Substantive advancement is now a general transfer theorem, and scope includes lattice graph geometry, graphical matrix obstructions, branched perfect forms, and certificate-search limitations. This is a materially stronger basis for a top-three comparison than the original specialized example. A defensible top-3/11 score is **not yet established**: the ten-paper randomized cohort and scoring sheets were not recovered, and this continuation has not independently rescored them. First-priority checking and external proof review are also still required. No editorial probability is claimed.
