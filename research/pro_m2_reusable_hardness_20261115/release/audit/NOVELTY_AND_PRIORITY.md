# Closest-source and novelty audit

Research date: 15 November 2026. This is a targeted primary-literature comparison, not an exhaustive first-priority certificate and not a venue-percentile score.

## Claimed new center of the revision

The central claim is a **fixed-block hardness-amplification theorem**: a finite positive normal-cone reserve and a certified ordinary-CP nonmember seed imply robust exponentially long improving adjacent paths from every bounded starting portfolio in the corresponding face. Finite integral shear types make N proportional to the required prefix m, with seed-dependent constants. Independent consequences concern the intrinsic graph geometry of the lattice epigraph. Graphical and SPN facts are explicitly used as antecedents, not repackaged as new classifications.

## 1. Perfect forms and local algorithms

**Dutour Sikirić–Schürmann–Vallentin**, *A simplex algorithm for rational cp-factorization*, Mathematical Programming 187 (2021), 25–45. DOI 10.1007/s10107-020-01467-4; arXiv:1807.01382. Primary manuscript inspected through arXiv HTML and inherited source notes. Their algorithm and normal-cone framework are the model here. Their Conjecture 3.2 is about termination for a suitable pivot rule, not polynomial pivot complexity. The revision does not claim to introduce perfect-form search or refute finite termination. Its new sufficient-conditions theorem covers every improving choice over a prescribed long prefix, bounded same-face portfolios, and richer certificate exits.

**Dannenberg–Schürmann**, *Perfect copositive matrices*, Communications in Mathematics 31(2) (2023), 137–156. DOI 10.46298/cm.11141. Official full paper: https://cm.episciences.org/11588/pdf . This studies perfect copositive matrices, their neighborhood graph, integer equivalence and the CP characterization by perfect forms. Shears and qualitative neighborhood geometry are not new here. The revision isolates a fixed SPD principal block, bounds all active denominator layers uniformly in position, and derives graph-growth and all-rule objective-amplification results. Those exact statements were not located in the inspected source. That is a scoped distinction, not a universal absence claim.

**Oertel–Schürmann**, *Generalized Perfect Matrices*, arXiv:2602.05841v2, revised 4 June 2026. Primary source: https://arxiv.org/html/2602.05841v2 . Generalized perfect matrices, the Interior Ryshkov property for rationally generated cones, and rational (non-)membership certificates are established there. Its proof explicitly uses simultaneous approximation. We therefore do not claim the first Diophantine argument or first generalized cone theorem. The finite shear-normal forms here give a uniform finite local alphabet, all-vertex denominator control, and an objective-independent intrinsic graph-growth theorem for the fixed-block epigraph, and are then used for the linear scale law.

## 2. Inhomogeneous lattice geometry

**Dutour Sikirić–Schürmann–Vallentin**, *Inhomogeneous extreme forms*, Annales de l'Institut Fourier 62 (2012), 2227–2255. DOI 10.5802/aif.2748; arXiv:1008.4751v2. Full HTML inspected: https://arxiv.org/html/1008.4751 . In particular Theorem 5.1 proves finiteness of perfect/eutactic inhomogeneous quadratic functions up to affine integral equivalence and scaling. We do not claim the first lattice-reduction finiteness theorem or the elementary fact that a connected periodic graph with finite quotient is quasi-isometric to its translation lattice.

The exact object here has **fixed H**, affine variables (b,c), all integral denominator layers y>=1, and an orthant restriction on numerator u. Uniformly bounded y is not built into the definition. The theorem treats the orthant boundary as well as the deep periodic region and proves exact degree-r growth of the entire bounded-edge graph, uniform denominators at every rational-block vertex, and bounded sheared heights for the full Ryshkov tangent rays. The application to all improving algorithms needs these quantitative uniform facts. Specialist priority checking of this fixed-block formulation remains appropriate before a first-theorem claim.

## 3. Comparison matrices and graphical seeds

**Drew–Johnson–Loewy**, *Completely positive matrices associated with M-matrices*, Linear and Multilinear Algebra 37 (1994), 303–310. DOI **10.1080/03081089408818334**. Publisher metadata verified. The comparison-matrix implication and triangle-free specialization are historical ingredients. Our manuscript gives direct proofs rather than relying on inaccessible details of the original full text.

**Kogan–Berman**, *Characterization of completely positive graphs*, Discrete Mathematics 114 (1993), 297–304. DOI 10.1016/0012-365X(93)90374-3. CP graphs and the obstruction by long odd cycles are classical. A seven-cycle instead of a five-cycle is not, by itself, asserted to be a new matrix-theoretic discovery.

**Barioli**, *Decreasing diagonal elements in completely positive matrices*, Rendiconti del Seminario Matematico della Università di Padova 100 (1998), 13–25. Full primary text inspected: https://www.numdam.org/item/RSMUP_1998__100__13_0.pdf . Section 3 distinguishes the PSD-minimal and CP-minimal diagonal value; Theorem 4.1 and Corollary 4.2 give bordered/excellent graph criteria, including the two-attachment case. Thus the existence of distinct PSD and CP hub thresholds is not new. The revision's Schur formulas are used to construct a whole certified-seed class. The stronger application is exact alignment with a perfect-form normal cone, collective deletion margins ensuring **all** proper principal submatrices are CP, preservation after a strictly positive CP reserve, and amplification against every improving rule. The multi-component and branched-root examples demonstrate that the hard inputs do not merely retain a smaller bad five-cycle principal block.

No first-priority claim is made for the general notion of a principal-minimal non-CP matrix. The relevance here is the explicit principal-minimal seed family satisfying the amplification certificate and its robust inability to be detected by any proper-principal separator test.

## 4. Diagonal and sparse corrections

**Shaked-Monderer**, *SPN graphs: When copositive = SPN*, LAA 509 (2016), 82–113. DOI 10.1016/j.laa.2016.07.018. Primary full manuscript: https://arxiv.org/html/1604.02172 . Theorem 5.4 handles forests and Corollary 5.3 handles graphs whose blocks have order at most four. The arrow decomposition and block-sum facts are credited as classical. The new implication is that confinement forces every stored prefix ray into the arrow subspace, so arbitrary diagonal correction and all corrected linear combinations fail simultaneously. Exact diagonal optimization cannot avoid this obstruction.

**Corrigendum**, LAA 541 (2018), 285–286; arXiv:1712.05115. Full correction inspected: https://arxiv.org/html/1712.05115 . It invalidates a lemma supporting the general triangular-book assertion, not the earlier forest/small-block facts used here. We do **not** rely on the corrected/withdrawn general book-graph statement. This matters because a careless citation of the 2016 abstract-level claims would overstate permissible correction masks.

**2025 author survey**, *CP graphs and SPN graphs*, Communications in Optimization Theory 2025, Article 3. DOI 10.23952/cot.2025.3; full primary PDF inspected at https://cot.mathres.org/issues/COT20253.pdf . It separates the settled CP graph characterization from the incomplete SPN classification and records the corrigendum. The manuscript makes no new full SPN classification claim.

## Adversarial restrictions established

- H=J_2 is strictly copositive but singular. Its epigraph has unbounded active-set size and a one-dimensional bounded-edge graph. Hence the fixed-SPD theorem cannot be extended verbatim to merely strict copositivity.
- J_3 is CP but has indefinite comparison matrix; triangle-freeness is necessary for the used converse.
- Same-color attachments make the Schur gap vanish; not every seed graph can produce a PD nonmember by changing one diagonal.
- A PD principal objective outside the perfect block's normal cone permits an improving escape direction. Positive definiteness alone is not a confinement hypothesis.
- The interiors of the normal cones of distinct perfect blocks are disjoint. The present mechanism cannot directly certify arbitrary different-block portfolios, although this is not an impossibility theorem for other mechanisms.
