# Primary-source boundary and novelty audit

Research date: 15 November 2026. This document distinguishes mathematically new assertions in this continuation from antecedents. It does not certify exhaustive historical first priority.

## 1. Perfect-matrix algorithm and geometry

Dutour Sikirić–Schürmann–Vallentin, *A simplex algorithm for rational cp-factorization*, Mathematical Programming 187 (2021), 25–45, DOI 10.1007/s10107-020-01467-4, arXiv:1807.01382. The simplex/perfect-form setting, active rank-one normals, adjacent transitions, and suitable-rule termination question are prior. Our theorem concerns a worst-case number of adjacent transitions, not a refutation of eventual suitable-rule termination.

Dannenberg–Schürmann, *Perfect Copositive Matrices*, Communications in Mathematics 31 (2023), 137–156, DOI 10.46298/cm.11141. The full primary article was inspected, especially Sections 6–7 and Lemma 7.2/Corollary 7.3 on faces of locally finite polyhedra. It studies perfect copositive forms, arithmetic equivalence, neighborhood graphs, and a characterization of CP. Those are not new. Its global arithmetic-equivalence discussion does not by itself give our finite signed active-vector box for each fixed positive-definite leading block, polynomial intrinsic graph growth, or quantified all-choice objective confinement. Primary full text: https://cm.episciences.org/11588/pdf .

Oertel–Schürmann, *Generalized Perfect Matrices*, arXiv:2602.05841v2, dated 4 June 2026 (the HTML document header also displays 24 August 2026). Its rationally generated-cone Interior Ryshkov theorem, generalized normal cones, polyhedral approximations, and rational certificate framework are explicit antecedents. We do not claim generalized qualitative perfectness. The new statement fixes one positive-definite principal block and obtains uniform normalized finite local models, not merely finiteness in a compact region or qualitative interiority. Primary full text: https://arxiv.org/html/2602.05841v2 .

## 2. Stronger correction tests: important classical antecedent

Naomi Shaked-Monderer, *SPN graphs: when copositive = SPN*, LAA 509 (2016), 82–113, DOI 10.1016/j.laa.2016.07.018. **Lemma 3.1 states exactly that a copositive matrix with a PSD principal submatrix of order n-1 is SPN.** The source proof and the preceding attribution to Dickinson–Dür–Gijben–Hildebrand were inspected. We give a self-contained convex-quadratic proof and explicitly credit the lemma. The contribution here is its algorithmic consequence: all PSD corrections of retained local rays, and broad history combinations, fail against the DNN targets. This is much stronger than our earlier scalar-identity calculation but is not marketed as a new decomposition theorem.

The corrigendum arXiv:1712.05115 was also checked. It identifies an error in Lemma 6.2 and resulting graph assertions in Theorems 6.4 and 9.2, not Lemma 3.1. None of those invalidated graph assertions is used. Primary sources: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1604.02172 and https://arxiv.org/pdf/1712.05115 .

## 3. Independent geometry and inhomogeneous forms

Erdahl, *A cone of inhomogeneous second-order polynomials*, Discrete & Computational Geometry 8 (1992), 387–416, DOI 10.1007/BF02293055, is an important antecedent for quadratic polynomials nonnegative on the integer lattice. Its role is acknowledged; a full independent proof audit of that entire article was not completed in this turn.

Dutour Sikirić–Schürmann–Vallentin, *Inhomogeneous extreme forms*, Annales de l'Institut Fourier 62 (2012), 2227–2255, DOI 10.5802/aif.2748. The primary full text was read around the definitions of inhomogeneous perfectness, the Erdahl cone, Theorems 2.4 and 2.6, and the central proof in Section 3. Their main conclusion characterizes local covering maxima by perfectness and eutaxy. It is not our graph-volume statement. Classical integer-lattice translation symmetry is an antecedent and is used only in the auxiliary unrestricted-integer epigraph.

The added content to audit for priority is the **orthant-truncated epigraph with all positive integer last-coordinate denominators**, including boundary vertices: a uniform Dirichlet gap bounds those denominators; KKT stationarity bounds the residual; shear normalization gives finite signed models; connectivity and a retained-active-set transfer yield an orthant-lattice embedding and Theta(R^r) intrinsic graph balls. An unrestricted denominator-one Delaunay diagram does not establish that whole chain. The proof expressly does not treat shears as symmetries of the whole orthant domain. Primary full text: https://www.numdam.org/item/10.5802/aif.2748.pdf .

## 4. New seed applications versus known graph obstructions

Motzkin–Straus, *Maxima for graphs and a new proof of a theorem of Turán*, Canadian Journal of Mathematics 17 (1965), 533–540, DOI 10.4153/CJM-1965-053-6, supplies the classical triangle-free copositive witness J-2 Adj(G). Odd-cycle obstructions and the CP-graph characterization excluding odd cycles of length at least five are classical (also described in the introduction of the SPN paper). We do not claim those as new.

The new applications establish simultaneously: exact exposure of the canonical leading block; the linear-scale all-rule lower bound; dense positive-definite targets; robust CP interiority of every proper principal submatrix; and failure of all proper-principal certificate tests. Orders 7,9,11 and a nonuniform weighted order-7 family were independently checked. The claim is not merely that more odd cycles are non-CP.

## 5. Current computational context

Oertel–Schürmann, *On Computing the Copositive Minimum and its Representatives*, arXiv:2509.23696 (2025), studies copositive-minimum computation and practical methods. Metadata and abstract were checked; no full-proof novelty comparison to this article is claimed. Our fixed-H local-cost statement does not contradict variable-dimensional complexity, and our enormous dimension-dependent template bounds are not proposed as a universally practical routine.

## 6. Exact novelty position

The strongest candidate new unit is the **finite-template / normal-cone lifting theorem**, together with the independent graph-growth consequence. It replaces one objective construction with a verifiable seed class and strengthens the scale to N=Theta(m). The PSD-correction extension and odd-cycle applications are substantive consequences, not separate claims of classical novelty.

No identical result was found in the primary sources inspected. That is a bounded search conclusion, not a proof that no older source contains it. External expert review and a broader monograph-level search remain appropriate. No publication rank, acceptance probability, or guaranteed novelty certification follows from this audit.
