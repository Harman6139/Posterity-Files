# PL research update — reusable hardness theory

**Mathematical outcome.** The manuscript now has a general finite-template/normal-cone lifting theorem, not just a five-cycle counterexample. For any fixed rational PD perfect leading block H, a rational PD strictly positive non-CP seed qualifies when its leading block lies in int V(H). All strictly improving adjacent rules require m steps after an explicit scaling N=Theta(m). The original construction used a high power of m. Complete local active/ray/neighbor computation is polynomial in bit height for fixed H, so the lower bound isolates the number of transitions rather than an expensive local oracle.

**Algorithmic strengthening.** Arbitrary nonnegative diagonal corrections and even arbitrary PSD corrections of all local rays are excluded, together with broad history combinations. The underlying SPN decomposition is classical and explicitly credited. Any fixed menu of starts on the same fixed-block face is covered; arbitrary different-block menus remain unresolved.

**New families.** Every odd order n>=5 has qualifying dense positive-definite nonmembers with all proper principal submatrices interior CP. This persists on a stated perturbation ball and blocks every proper-principal certificate test. Nonuniform weighted cycles also qualify. Higher odd orders are not padded five-cycle obstructions.

**Independent scope.** For every real H>0, the fixed-block epigraph has a connected bounded-degree vertex graph, Theta(R^r) intrinsic graph-ball growth, and a quasi-isometrically embedded orthant lattice. Rational H gives exponentially distant vertices with logarithmic encodings. This applies without a separating objective or five-cycle seed.

**Checks.** The independent exact suite passed five seed families, 39 proper-principal factorizations, 129 full tangent rays, 300 graph-adjacency comparisons, and seven corruption rejections. The original suite passed unchanged. For an order-five analytic prefix of 2^64 pivots, the new N uses 575 bits rather than 4,823; no long trajectory was simulated.

**Positioning.** Both reusable scope and substantive strength have increased substantially. The paper is now a credible candidate for a fresh top-three comparison, but no top-three rank is certified: the reported randomized ten-paper cohort and complete rubric were unavailable. Do not replace the old rankings with invented ones. Closest primary sources and the exact novelty boundary are recorded, including the classical SPN lemma and inhomogeneous-lattice antecedents. External proof review and exhaustive first-priority certification remain undone.
