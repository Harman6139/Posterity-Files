## Deliberately limited or unresolved

- Arbitrary portfolios using different leading perfect blocks, input-dependent distant starts, nonlocal jumps, non-descending moves, and unrelated global separator searches are not covered.
- The strict normal-reserve hypothesis is a sufficient condition. No complete classification of all hard objectives is claimed. Examples show why simply dropping it fails for this proof.
- Positive definite H cannot be replaced by merely strictly copositive singular H in the finite-type/growth theorem. A rank-one H example has unbounded active cardinality and growth degree below its order.
- Triangle-free comparison necessity cannot be extended to all graphs. Same-color hub attachments have no PD/non-CP interval.
- No practical or dimension-uniform polynomial algorithm for seed discovery/copolyhedral verification is claimed. The large constants and finite template lists are explicit, not optimized.
- The new results have written analytical proofs and separate exact instance checks, not independent human review or formal proof-assistant certification.
- Targeted primary-source comparison has been completed and close antecedents explicitly credited. Exhaustive historical first priority is not certified.
- The user's reported 4th/11 advancement and 7th/11 scope ranking is a baseline report, not a new measurement. The ten-paper random cohort and full scoring sheets were not recovered. No revised top-3 rank is assigned or guaranteed.
