## Mathematical outcome

The core new theorem is a finite-template result for fixed-positive-definite-block quadratic epigraphs. It bounds all normalized active vectors and full tangent rays, yields a general verifiable normal-cone seed criterion with linear scale N=Theta(m), and isolates the number of pivots from the cost of a local step. The independent graph consequence is connected bounded-degree intrinsic graphs with Theta(R^r) ball growth and an embedded orthant lattice.

The stronger local model allows arbitrary PSD (hence nonnegative diagonal) corrections and specified history certificates, and multiple starts in a bounded region of the same fixed-block face. All proper-principal certificate tests fail for the robust odd-order families, whose every proper principal submatrix is interior CP. Larger odd orders are not padded five-cycle obstructions.

The PSD-principal-block-to-SPN lemma is classical (Shaked-Monderer, LAA 509 (2016), Lemma 3.1) and is credited. The associated corrigendum concerns other lemmas/theorems, which are not used. The new claims are the finite-template/lifting theorem and its quantified consequences, not the classical SPN lemma or the existence of odd-cycle obstructions.

## Remaining limits

Arbitrary menus of starts with different leading blocks, arbitrary nonextreme feasible descent directions, and unrestricted nonlocal certificates remain outside the lower bound. Strict exposure is a sufficient criterion, not a classification of all hard seeds. Counterexamples show why the present confinement proof cannot simply drop these assumptions.

No external human review, proof-assistant formalization, exhaustive first-priority certification, or revised top-three benchmark rank is claimed. The user-reported randomized ten-paper cohort was not available for a defensible reranking. The revision materially strengthens advancement and scope and is a candidate for reassessment against the unchanged sample. Original theorem files and baseline branches remain unchanged.
