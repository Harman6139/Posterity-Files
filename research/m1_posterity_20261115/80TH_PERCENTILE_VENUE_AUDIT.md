# Journal screen: upper-quintile mathematical substance, not acceptance probability

**Assessment date:** 2026-11-15.  
**Recommendation:** After independent proof and originality certification, first submit to **Advances in Nonlinear Analysis (ANA)**. **Bulletin of the London Mathematical Society (BLMS)** is the stronger broad-prestige stretch venue, but has a **20-page limit** and higher cross-area exposition requirement. This recommendation is conditional; the strict 8/10 upper-quintile gate is **NOT PASSED** by the current evidence.

## 1. Fixed protocol; no post-hoc attractive comparators

The protocol was fixed in research Checkpoint 13 BEFORE sampling: first five original-research papers in publisher order in each of two issues per venue, excluding surveys, errata, editorials, and issue boilerplate. ANA 13(1) (2024) and 14(1) (2025); BLMS 56(1) (2024) and 57(1) (2025). Ten papers **per** venue. This is a reproducible *issue-position* sample, not random sampling of all accepted papers and not a meaningful estimate of unconditional acceptance probability.

For each comparison the predeclared substantive dimensions (0–5 each) are: novelty N (.25), proof depth D (.25), generality/reuse scope G (.20), cross-field reuse R (.15), clarity C (.15). Audience fit F is a separate axis. A completed gate demands **correctness+priority**, inspection of full main statements and proofs where accessible, and evidence favoring the submitted work against at least **8/10** sampled papers for a journal. Scores are deliberately NOT manufactured where full proof reading or independent verification is incomplete.

**Critical decision:** 20 DOIs and theorem-level distinctions are collected below; full results and proofs have not been audited for all 20. The M1 nonlinear resonance's uniform analytic remainder and rational threshold have not received external-style independent verification, and comprehensive historical priority is not certified. Accordingly, the paper CANNOT legitimately be called *proven upper-20%* or assigned an 8/10 success count.

## 6. Novelty/prior art and the risk of a premature submission

Close antecedents:
- Bolte–Gaubert–Vigeral, **Definable zero-sum stochastic games**, Math. Oper. Res. (2015), DOI 10.1287/moor.2014.0666: tame discounted curves, first-order normalized iteration; not the displayed natural-scale conic last-step theorem.
- Liu–Ryu–Yin, **A New Use of Douglas–Rachford Splitting and ADMM for Identifying Infeasible, Unbounded, and Pathological Conic Programs**, Math. Program. (2019), DOI 10.1007/s10107-018-1318-4 (verify metadata before submission): classical weak-infeasibility and unscaled displacement; not the claimed circle.
- Atenas, **Understanding the Douglas–Rachford splitting method through the lenses of Moreau-type envelopes**, Comput. Optim. Appl. 90 (2025), DOI 10.1007/s10589-024-00646-9: weakly convex and convergence/linear rates from an envelope, a different regime.
- Dinh–Jansen–Luke, **Cyclic Relaxed Douglas–Rachford Splitting for Inconsistent Nonconvex Feasibility**, J. Optim. Theory Appl. 209 (2026), DOI 10.1007/s10957-026-02996-2: relaxed cyclic/nonconvex, local convergence, different iteration.
- Do–Jentzen–Kuckuck, arXiv:2610.29211v2 (2026): finite spherical length of **continuous definable gradient trajectories**, NOT a new discrete DRS claim.
- Pazy, Bauschke–Douglas–Moursi, Ryu, and the large monotone-operator/SDP literature require detailed final priority audit.

Recent targeted searches did not locate the exact rational resonance family, but this is negative search evidence, not novelty proof. Full bibliographic metadata should be rechecked before any public submission.
