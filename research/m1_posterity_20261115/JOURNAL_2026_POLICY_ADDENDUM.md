# 2026 venue-policy addendum — primary publisher verification

_Date: 2026-11-15. Complements, rather than rewrites, the predeclared 20-paper benchmark._

## ANA cost and length

The publisher's **Article Processing Charges**, effective December 2025, says the accepted-paper APC is **EUR 1,300**, with **no submission fee** and waiver/discount arrangements. Funding and ability to pay are explicitly separate from editorial assessment. Primary source: https://www.degruyterbrill.com/publication/journal_key/ANONA/downloadAsset/ANONA_Article%20Processing%20Charges.pdf

Its **Instructions for Authors**, effective December 2025, explicitly include convex analysis, nonsmooth analysis, optimization, set-valued functions and variational inequalities. The journal page says there is **no specific manuscript length restriction**, though concise clear presentation is encouraged. Primary sources:
- https://www.degruyterbrill.com/publication/journal_key/ANONA/downloadAsset/ANONA_Instructions%20for%20Authors.pdf
- https://www.degruyterbrill.com/journal/key/anona/html

The archival ten-paper ANA sample in `80TH_PERCENTILE_VENUE_AUDIT.md` has **median final acceptance of 261 days (~8.57 months)**. It comprises only accepted articles selected by issue order, includes actual revisions and is NOT a forecast for a new paper.

## BLMS decision speeds vs acceptance odds

The LMS official 2025 policy-update page reports **median submission-to-FIRST-decision 29 days for BLMS** in 2025 year-to-date (37 days in 2023). It does NOT equate this to median time to *final acceptance*; the web article does not give a BLMS final-acceptance median. The cited drop to 265 days of submission-to-acceptance refers to **JLMS**, not BLMS. Primary source: https://www.lms.ac.uk/BLMSJLMS-2025update

The BLMS peer-review page reports that in 2024 around **67% of papers SENT TO FULL REVIEW** were ultimately accepted. This is **conditional on surviving initial editorial screening** and is NOT a 67% overall submission acceptance rate, nor is it a probability applicable to our draft. It also states about 90% of preliminary editorial decisions occurred within two months in 2024. Primary source: https://www.lms.ac.uk/publications/blms/peerreview

BLMS research articles are at most **20 pages**, with particular emphasis on a broad comprehensible motivation for readers outside the immediate area. Primary source: https://www.lms.ac.uk/publications/blms/submission

## Decision

1. For **best actual mathematical fit with the current full manuscript**, **Advances in Nonlinear Analysis** remains the stronger target, conditional on independent mathematical and originality checks.
2. For **maximum broad-mathematics prestige** among the currently sampled outlets, **BLMS** is an appropriate stretch. The manuscript needs a <=20-page typeset form and a broad opening; its counterexample and replacement principle need referee verification.
3. **No venue guarantees eight-month acceptance.** A short first decision is different from a favorable final decision; journal acceptance-rate statements conditioned on peer review are not unconditional submission chances.
4. **Upper 20% of published-paper substance remains NOT CERTIFIED** due to incomplete full-paper and proof comparison, despite the 20 DOI sample.

This addendum makes no legal, financial or publisher-price guarantee: check the live journal submission system before paying or submitting.
